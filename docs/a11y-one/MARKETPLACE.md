## Product name
A11y One — Accessible Client Theme (WCAG 2.2 AA)

## Short tagline (one line)
The accessible client area theme for WHMCS — WCAG 2.2 AA, screen‑reader and keyboard friendly, built on Twenty‑One.

## Summary (listing intro)
A11y One makes your WHMCS client area genuinely usable for everyone — including customers who rely on
a screen reader, keyboard, or high‑contrast display. It is a drop‑in child theme of the standard
Twenty‑One theme, so it keeps the familiar WHMCS look and inherits future Twenty‑One updates, while
bringing every client‑facing page up to **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**. A matching accessible order form is
included.

Accessibility here is not just a passing automated scan: A11y One was designed and tested from the
real keyboard and screen‑reader experience — clear page titles and headings, fully operable menus,
named controls, visible focus, and proper landmarks — so disabled customers can actually sign up,
pay invoices, open tickets, and manage their services on their own.

## Key features
- **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** across the entire client area, verified with automated (axe‑core) and manual
  screen‑reader/keyboard testing.
- **Child of Twenty‑One** — no core or parent edits; keeps your current look and upgrades cleanly.
- **Accessible order form** (`A11y Cart`) — accessible product configuration, domain search, and
  checkout.
- **Meaningful page titles and headings** on every page (services, domains, billing, support,
  account, and more).
- **Keyboard‑operable navigation** — top menus work as proper arrow‑key menus; a skip link and
  labelled landmarks let assistive‑technology users jump straight to content.
- **Accessible components** — operable form controls, keyboard‑friendly data tables, an accessible
  support‑ticket rating, labelled rich‑text editor and file uploads, copy‑to‑clipboard with
  announcements, and focus‑managed dialogs.
- **AA‑contrast colors and visible focus** throughout; status is always conveyed by text, not color
  alone.
- **English and Turkish** out of the box, fully translatable.
- **A published, VPAT‑style accessibility conformance report** you can share with customers or
  procurement teams.

## Why it matters
Accessibility is increasingly a legal and procurement requirement (e.g. EN 301 549, ADA, the
European Accessibility Act). An inaccessible client area can lock out customers and create
compliance risk. A11y One gives you a ready, documented, AA‑conformant client experience without a
custom build.

## Compatibility
- WHMCS 9.x
- Parent theme: Twenty‑One (included with WHMCS)
- Works in current Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge

## What is included
- The `A11y One` client theme
- The `A11y Cart` accessible order form
- English + Turkish language files
- Installation guide, conformance report, and changelog

## Installation (summary)
1. Upload the `a11y-one` and `a11y-cart` template folders to your WHMCS `templates` directories.
2. Merge the included language keys into your site‑level `lang/overrides` files.
3. Set A11y One as the client theme and A11y Cart as the order form, then clear the template cache.

Full step‑by‑step instructions are included in the download.
